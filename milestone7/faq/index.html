FAQ

What card games do you carry?

We focus on Magic: The Gathering, Pokemon, and Yu-Gi-Oh! You can browse singles by game from the navigation menu.

Do I need an account to place an order?

No, creating a free account allows you to save shipping details and track orders, but you can still checkout as a guest.

How do you grade card condition?

Singles are listed as Near Mint, Lightly Played, Moderately Played, or Heavily Played.

How does shipping work?

Orders are shipped in sleeves, or small boxes depending on size and value.

Do you buy cards or accept trades?

Your collections and singles can be purchased for store credit, but trades are not accepted.

What if my order is wrong or arrives damaged?

That's your problem, not ours.
